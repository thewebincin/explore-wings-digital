Place your transparent client logo PNG files in this folder (`assets/logos/`):

- lulu-mall.png
- munch.png
- kubaba.png
- kochi.png
- travancore-royal.png
- kovalam-palace.png
- kasavu-mahal.png
- villa-maya.png
- aura-aesthetics.png
- spice-harbour.png
- malabar-crest.png
- trivandrum-academy.png

Until a PNG file is added, the website automatically displays a styled luxury wordmark fallback for that brand.
You can add or remove brands anytime in `script.js` under `const BRANDS_LIST`.
