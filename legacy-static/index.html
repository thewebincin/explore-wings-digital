03 — Our Process

Four simple steps from first call to live site.

Clear milestones
01

Listen

We sit down with you in person in Trivandrum or over a video call. We study what your business actually sells, what customers ask for, and where your current setup is falling short.

Outcome: Project scope & fixed quote
02

Create

We design and build the work with weekly check-ins. If you need photography, we visit your premises with lights and cameras. You review real working screens, not static mockups.

Outcome: Working staging preview
03

Launch

We test every link, contact form, WhatsApp button, and mobile screen size across iPhone and Android devices. We configure Google Search Console and deploy on fast servers.

Outcome: Live website & assets handed over
04

Grow

Going live is step one. We monitor site speed, keep Google Maps rankings active, and run targeted ads when you are ready to expand your customer reach.

Outcome: Ongoing stability & inquiries
